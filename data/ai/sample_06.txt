Everyone has some activity that they enjoy doing in their free time. My favorite hobby is listening to music. I usually listen to music when I am travelling, studying or relaxing at home.

Music helps me feel relaxed when I am tired or stressed. Sometimes I also listen to songs while doing other activities. Different types of songs can change my mood and make me feel better.

I think having a hobby is important because it gives us a break from our daily routine. It also helps us spend our free time in a useful and enjoyable way.
