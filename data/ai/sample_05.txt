Technology has become a major part of our daily life. From the time we wake up until we go to sleep, we use technology in many ways. Mobile phones, computers and the internet have made our lives easier.

Students can use the internet to learn new things and find information within a few seconds. We can also communicate with people who are far away from us. Technology is also useful in hospitals, banks, transport and many other fields.

However, using technology too much can also cause problems. Many people spend several hours on their phones without realizing the time. So, technology is useful when we use it in the right way. It depends on how we choose to use it.
