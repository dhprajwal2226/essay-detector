The environment is very important for every living thing. We need clean air, water, trees and land to live a healthy life. But today, pollution and deforestation are becoming serious problems.

People throw plastic and other waste in public places, which makes the surroundings dirty. Cutting too many trees also affects animals and increases pollution. These problems may become worse if we do not take proper care of nature.

We can all do small things to protect the environment. We can plant trees, avoid unnecessary plastic, save water and keep our surroundings clean. If everyone takes responsibility, we can make the Earth a better place to live.
