One Piece isn't like other anime — it shows the real image of the real world. The government and the pirates in that anime exactly show the system in the real world, like people and the government. The government is corrupted, involved in all the bad things, discriminating, having the power and misusing it in all the possible ways. Then we see the pirates, who are good, not on the evil side, fighting against the government to show the reality of the world which the government tried to cover up.
